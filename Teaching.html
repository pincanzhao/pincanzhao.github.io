Teaching Philosophy

I believe the best way to teach demanding engineering subjects is to turn abstract concepts into intuition that students can reason with, by grounding every idea in concrete, real-world problems they can build and test.

My teaching spans graduate and undergraduate levels at four institutions, including serving as a course instructor at Western University and teaching in an English-taught UK joint program. I value clear problem framing, hands-on labs, and mentoring students from diverse backgrounds.
